# Runtime, visual and evaluation data

- `go2-v0.1.0-runtime.zip`: 38 mandatory runtime files preserving their relative paths: seven frozen PPO weights plus normalization/configuration, two fitted heads, selector bank, native meshes and three representative raw reference trajectories. See `release/assets.json` for each file's size and SHA.
- `go2-v0.1.0-visuals.zip`: fourteen optional files (seven existing clips and their posters). Extract at the repository root to view the preserved rc1 HTML. No new video or experiment was generated for this release.
- `results/`: selected original fixed-matrix, component and paired-training result tables. Their exact bytes and source paths are documented in `results/provenance.json`.
- `docs/releases/go2-157-v1-rc1/`: retained historical preparation records. Pending visual/publication fields there describe that historical snapshot; current results and visual scope are in README.md and RESULTS.md.

The complete mother-workspace history, all intermediate training artifacts and full-pipeline training reproduction are not included in this engineering source preview. Python/wheels/venv and private conversations are excluded. No original evidence has been deleted.
